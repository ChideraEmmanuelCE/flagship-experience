A few useful answers

Before we begin.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the agreed scope, your content and current availability. You receive a proposed schedule with the quote before making a start payment.

Will I need a monthly subscription?

There is no mandatory development subscription in these packages. Hosting, domain renewals and any optional paid tools have their own costs, explained in the quote. Ongoing support is optional and scoped separately.

Can customers pay directly on my website?

Payment integration can be quoted separately after we check your provider and account activation. The catalogue package uses enquiry links; it does not include checkout or collect money from your customers.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, logo if you have one, service descriptions, contact details and images you have permission to use. The quote will specify any help with copy or content.

Who owns the website?

The handover includes the custom source files after the agreed final payment. You control your domain and hosting accounts. Any third-party assets and software keep their original licences, which will be identified during handover.
